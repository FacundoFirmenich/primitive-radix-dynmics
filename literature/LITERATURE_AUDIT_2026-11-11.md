## Novelty boundary

No claim is made that Möbius number systems, radix dynamics, rational periodic points, projective matrix representations or symbolic codings are new. The paper's claim is the specific coupled radix/Möbius-conjugate system, its primitive-content cocycle formulation and the proved sharp small-cardinality rigidity threshold.

The final external release should repeat this audit against MathSciNet and zbMATH immediately before submission.
