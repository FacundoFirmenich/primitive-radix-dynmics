# RC7/closure claim boundary

The branch may claim:

- exact five-state combinatorial reduction to twenty generator-unoriented factor-specific normal forms;
- exact uniqueness of the strict edge at cardinality five;
- the explicit five-state CRT realization;
- near-coprime splitting for the declared dense forms;
- elliptic reduction and Siegel-finiteness for `DF015-0015`.

The branch must not claim, until separately proved:

- complete arithmetic classification of all five-state forms;
- `19/20` impossibility;
- global uniqueness of the known state tuple;
- the globally least realizing base;
- effective enumeration of the nineteen elliptic candidate sets;
- blanket factor-2 mod-8 or factor-3 mod-9 impossibility.
