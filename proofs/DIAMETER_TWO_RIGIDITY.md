# Directed-diameter-two rigidity

For a reduced state (x=p/q),
[
operatorname{den}(T_Bx)=q/gcd(q,B),qquad
operatorname{num}(S_Bx)=p/gcd(p,B).
]

If a (T)-edge is strict, its target has denominator at most (q/2) and therefore, in the high domain, numerator (<q/2<p). No one-step return can restore both primitive coordinates. For a two-step return, the words (TT,SS,TS,ST) each fail monotonically: (TT) cannot restore the denominator; (SS) cannot restore the numerator; after (TS) the numerator remains below the source numerator; after (ST) the denominator remains below the source denominator.

Thus no strict edge can belong to a strongly connected graph of directed diameter at most two. Every edge is unreduced; on a finite set both endomaps are injective and therefore permutations.

Authority: symbolic proof. Supports C007 and the (n=2,3) part of C008.
