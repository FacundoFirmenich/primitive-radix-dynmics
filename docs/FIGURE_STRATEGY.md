# Figure strategy

All figures are regenerated by `src/figures/generate_figures.py` from exact constants/certificates.

1. `fig01_object_architecture` — rational dynamics -> primitive projective lift -> content cocycle.
2. `fig02_sharp_threshold` — rigidity at n=2,3,4 and first exact nonpermutation at n=5.
3. `fig03_counterfamily_graph` — exact five-state T/S graph; strict factor-3 edge emphasized.
4. `fig04_spectral_cycle` — periodic word, matrix product and defect eigenvalue identity.
5. `graphical_abstract` — compact integrated visual.

Figures are exported as SVG (editable/vector), PDF (publication vector) and PNG (web/preview).
