# Exact content-cycle identity

**2026-11-11 · PROVED_SYMBOLIC · Claim C016**

Let C be any directed cycle with rational states x_i=p_i/q_i and local content defects g_i on T-edges and h_i on S-edges. Then

R_C = product_{T edges i} (x_{i+1}/x_i) = product_{S edges i} (x_i/x_{i+1}).

Proof: on a T-edge p_{i+1}/p_i = (x_{i+1}/x_i)/g_i, and on an S-edge p_{i+1}/p_i=1/h_i. Multiplying the numerator ratios around the cycle yields 1=(product_T x_{i+1}/x_i)/R_C. The total product of all x_{i+1}/x_i around a cycle is 1, giving the second equality.
