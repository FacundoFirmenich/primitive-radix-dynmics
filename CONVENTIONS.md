# Conventions

- Strict high domain: \(\mathcal H=(1/2,1)\).
- States are reduced positive rationals \(p/q\).
- `T` and `S` use the canonical RC1 labeling recorded in `certificates/ARITHMETIC_PROGRESSION.json`.
- Canonical five-state maps: `T=[1,0,3,2,4]`, `S=[4,2,1,0,0]`.
- Unique strict edge in the realized counterfamily: `S_3: x3 -> x0`.
- A finite exhaustive computation is labelled `EXACT_CERTIFICATE` or `FINITE_EXHAUSTIVE`, never `PROVED_GLOBAL` unless a theorem reduces the global claim to that finite domain.
- Dates use ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Hashes use SHA-256.
