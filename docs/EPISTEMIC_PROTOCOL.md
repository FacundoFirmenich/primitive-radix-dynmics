# RGΦ-I epistemic protocol

Adapted from healthy practices audited in `FacundoFirmenich/crispr-observability`.

1. Separate reading order from proof order.
2. Use explicit AND gates for conjunctive claims.
3. Bind every promoted claim to symbolic proof, exact certificate, CRT realization or declared replay.
4. Attach an explicit revision condition to every strong claim.
5. Preserve adverse outcomes, open branches and `NOT_CLAIMED`.
6. Layout, colour and proximity are navigation only, never evidential strength.
7. Bind reviewer artifacts to an exact scientific-authority commit.
8. Keep manuscript privacy separate from proof/checker transparency.
9. Build receipts certify source/graph integrity, not peer review.
10. Preserve actual artifact bytes; a hash is an identity, not a substitute.

Authority order:

`symbolic proof > exact finite certificate > independent replay > discovery computation`.
